Frequently Asked Questions

Minimum capital requirement for Dubai Free Zone company? expand_more

Capital requirements vary significantly depending on the specific Free Zone and the type of activity. Some require no upfront capital, while others may require AED 50,000 to AED 300,000. Our consultants will guide you to the most efficient option based on your business model.

Can I own 100% of my company in mainland Dubai? expand_more

Yes. Recent legislative changes in the UAE allow for 100% foreign ownership in mainland companies for over 1,000 commercial and industrial activities. A local sponsor is no longer mandatory for these specific activities.

How long does the company formation process take? expand_more

Standard Free Zone setups can be completed in as little as 3-5 working days once all documentation is submitted. Mainland setups typically take 1-3 weeks depending on the required external approvals.

Do I need a physical office for my license? expand_more

It depends on the jurisdiction. Many Free Zones offer 'Flexi-desk' or virtual office solutions which satisfy the legal requirement for a lease agreement. Mainland licenses generally require a physical office space.
